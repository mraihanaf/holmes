## What to build

- Solve **one of the 14 official NASA Space Apps 2026 challenges**.
- Challenge tabs contain objectives and resources.
- Teams have only the **two-day event**. Judges do **not** expect a fully finished solution. Show a **solution blueprint** and explain design reasoning even if the project is incomplete.

## Team

- Join or create a team at the Jakarta local-event page: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/jakarta/?tab=teams
- There is a "looking for team" WhatsApp group.

## Project demo (required)

Choose **one**:
- **7-slide presentation** (a title slide counts), or
- **30-second video**.

Upload to a public site (YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub, OneDrive, Dropbox) and put **one** link in the "Link to Project Demo" field. No password, permission, or registration. Test with a logged-out browser.

Demo tips from the deck:
- Judges look at demos first.
- Pick the format early and decide "what story does my team want to tell?"
- Use strong visuals and readable text; limit words per slide.
- Delegate one teammate to lead the demo.
- Slides/projects in **English**. Videos may be in a native language **with English subtitles**.
- Slide deck: max 7 slides. Video: max 30 seconds.

Slide deck should include:
- Team name and member names
- Challenge
- Image of the project
- Description of the user experience
- How space-agency open data and technology make the solution possible

Video story arc (optional):
- 0:00 introduce yourself/team and challenge
- 0:05 name the solution, problem, opportunity
- 0:10 describe the idea, how it works, user experience, and how NASA data made it possible
- 0:20 look into the future: what will the idea change?
- 0:30 end

## Project submission page fields

- **Project name**
- **High-level summary** (50–200 words): what you developed, how it addresses the challenge, why it matters.
- **Link to Project Demo** (one link only).
- **Project details:** what it does, how it works, benefits, goals, tools/languages/hardware/software.
- **NASA data:** which NASA data/resource you used and how; it inspired the project.
- **Space agency partner data and other references:** optional partner data, plus all other data/resources/tools. List every resource, including open-source code, text, and images.
- **Permitted AI use:** LLMs, chatbots, image/video generation, foundation models, coding assistants are allowed. Disclose where/how. Generated images/videos need a visible watermark; generated code/data need acknowledgment in text and metadata. No NASA branding in generated content.
- **Citations:** every source must be cited, even open source. NASA can remove nominations/awards for violations.
- Uploads: JPG/PNG/GIF up to 10 MB on the page; videos/documents must be external links.

## Judging criteria

1. **Impact** — quality and quantity of impact; big or small problem; inspires/helps many or few.
2. **Creativity** — novel vs incremental; creative approach.
3. **Validity** — scientifically valid; will it do what it says; can it work in the real world?
4. **Relevance** — responsive to the challenge; complete solution vs long way to go; technically feasible; usable/user-friendly.
5. **Presentation** — how well the team tells the story of the challenge, solution, and why it matters.

## Eligibility for global judging

1. Respond to one of the official 2026 challenges (not "Create Your Own Challenge").
2. All team members registered and identified on the team's Project Page. No new members after submission closes.
3. Complete all required fields.
4. English on the project submission page and linked materials (video exception: native language + English subtitles).
5. Submit before the deadline.
6. Follow the Participant Terms and Conditions.
7. Check the box confirming you read the submission requirements.
8. Check the box confirming original work and that all external resources are listed in References.

## Practical implications for us

- Start the demo/story early; the submission form is part of the judging surface.
- Keep a running `references.md` from hour one: every dataset, article, library, image, and AI tool.
- Use at least one **NASA resource from the challenge's Resources tab** to qualify for global awards.
- The project page must be in English; our internal notes can be in Indonesian.
- AI is allowed; disclose it. Do not generate NASA logos/branding.
- The 7-slide deck is probably the safer demo format because it is easier to iterate and to make accessible.
